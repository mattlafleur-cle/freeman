The Mantra

Coach Marcus Freeman and Leave No Doubt

After ten straight wins to close the 2025 regular season, Notre Dame was the first team left out of the College Football Playoff. Instead of leaving the decision to the committee next time, Coach Freeman gave his team a simple standard for 2026: leave no doubt.

These shirts are for the fans who bought in: students, alumni, families, and anyone who wants to wear that standard on game day and every day after.

See the shirts

“It’s up to us to leave no doubt.”

Marcus Freeman, quoted by the Associated Press, January 2026

Seven of the nine shirts come in XS through 5XL. The forest green 2026 Script and forest green Chest Shield come in S through 4XL. You choose your size on the product page at ndshirt.com.
